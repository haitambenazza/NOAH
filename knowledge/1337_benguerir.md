# 1337 Coding School Overview (Thirteen Thirty Seven)
1337 is Morocco's tuition-free computer science and coding school (named after leetspeak "1337"), launched by OCP Group and UM6P as part of the global 42 Network inspired by Ecole 42. Campuses operate in Khouribga (2018), Ben Guerir (2019), Tetouan (1337 Med), and Rabat.

## 1337 Ben Guerir Campus and Home City (Benguerir, Bengruir, UM6P)
We live in Ben Guerir (Benguerir), Morocco, home to Mohammed VI Green City and UM6P. The 1337 Ben Guerir campus opened in 2019 beside UM6P and runs 24/7 with 300 iMac workstations across 2 open Clusters, high-speed internet, 2 Playgrounds, and 8 meeting rooms for clubs and teamwork.

## 1337 Education System, Pedagogy, and La Piscine
1337 is 100% free for ages 18 to 30 with no prior degree, no teachers, and no lectures. Students advance through gamified peer-to-peer projects and peer evaluations, starting with the Common Core (C, Unix, C++, algorithms, networking, web) before specializing in AI, cybersecurity, or systems. Admission is via La Piscine (The Pool), an intensive 4-week coding bootcamp.

## 1337 Chairmen, Directors, Leadership, and Bocal Staff
Key leadership: Mostafa Terrab (Chairman and CEO of OCP Group, founding sponsor of 1337 and founding President of UM6P), Hicham El Habti (President of UM6P), and Larbi El Hilali (General Director of 1337 Morocco). At 1337 Ben Guerir, Yassir Boux leads technical and pedagogical operations alongside the Bocal (the 24/7 pedagogical, technical, and campus staff team).
